Capacitor-Based Beam-Switching 2×2 Patch Array Antenna

Undergraduate thesis research on reconfigurable antenna systems and two-dimensional beam steering.

01

Research objective

The work investigated a 2×2 patch array capable of steering the main beam along both X and Y axes, using a strip-slot feeding network and capacitor-based switching.
