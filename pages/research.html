Research Works

UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · 2025

Capacitor-Based Beam-Switching 2×2 Patch Array Antenna Using Strip-Slot Power Divider

My undergraduate thesis focused on the design and investigation of a 2×2 microstrip patch array antenna capable of two-dimensional beam switching.

The research explored the use of a capacitor-based switching mechanism to control the beam direction of the antenna. The design incorporated a strip-slot power divider, inverted T-shaped slot line, quarter-wavelength microstrip stub and hybrid junction.

OPERATING FREQUENCY 10 GHz
ANTENNA ARRAY 2 × 2 Patch Array
SWITCHING METHOD Capacitor / Varactor
SIMULATED S11 Approximately −52 dB
Keysight ADS CST MATLAB EM Simulation
