UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · 2025

Capacitor-Based Beam-Switching 2×2 Patch Array Antenna Using Strip-Slot Power Divider

My undergraduate thesis focused on the design and investigation of a 2×2 microstrip patch array antenna capable of two-dimensional beam switching.

The research investigated capacitor-based switching techniques for controlling the radiation characteristics of the antenna. The design incorporated a strip-slot power divider, inverted T-shaped slot line, quarter-wavelength microstrip stub and hybrid junction.

FREQUENCY 10 GHz
ARRAY 2 × 2 Patch Array
SWITCHING Capacitor / Varactor
SIMULATED S11 ≈ −52 dB
